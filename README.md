## Mathematics · Algebra

[Exact nonnegative rank of a twelve-state composition tensor](mathematics/algebra/twelve-state-composition-tensor/)  
26 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

The nonnegative rank of a specified twelve-element monoid's composition tensor is 35. The paper gives the decomposition, matching lower bounds, and an exact verifier.

## Mathematics · Discrete mathematics

[A three-bit boundary for cyclic Boolean–modular inversion](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/three-bit-cyclic-inversion/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Three initial bits determine every preimage of a cyclic Boolean–modular map. The complete inverse uses at most eight boundary assignments and 8w local bit updates. An explicit periodic family proves the eight-preimage bound sharp.

## Mathematics · Affine inverse fibers

## Analysis

### Cyclic-Shift Energies: Minimal State and Cyclotomic Gaps

[Paper and exact source](mathematics/analysis/cyclic-shift-energies/README.md) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23897127) · [Release](https://github.com/lmtlssss/math/releases/tag/cyclic-shift-energies-v1.0.0)

A cyclotomic divisibility criterion decides equality in an integer-occupancy spectral bound. At prime periods, algebraic norms yield a quantitative positive gap for nonflat spectra. The bound is attained at exactly five occupancies in a complete 210-state example. Minimal linear state and retained-potential identities supply the representation framework.

### Robust Amplitude Elimination and Exact Lattice-Phase Selection

[Paper and exact source](mathematics/analysis/homogeneous-amplitude-elimination/README.md) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23897131) · [Release](https://github.com/lmtlssss/math/releases/tag/homogeneous-amplitude-elimination-v1.0.0)

A complete exact selection law for a translating common lattice reduces finite robust designs to residue cells and affine envelopes. It gives rational selection probabilities, expected optima and boundary decisions. A four-design example separates the continuous winner from the phase-selection probabilities. A second theorem proves persistent reversal under arbitrarily fine lattice refinement.

### Sharp Observation Bounds and Minimal Peak Horizons for Damped Recurrences

[Paper and exact source](mathematics/analysis/damped-recurrence-certificates/README.md) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23900237) · [Release](https://github.com/lmtlssss/math/releases/tag/damped-recurrence-certificates-v1.0.1)

Exact coefficient inequalities classify the stable conjugate-root recurrences with minimal uniform peak horizon two or three. A symmetric observation hexagon supplies necessity and sufficiency. An explicit rational boundary example has shortest uniform horizon three. Sharp observation constants, rational adaptive certificates and exact Stein sums accompany the new coefficient-region result.

### Affine Cycles: Exact Cut Defects and Minimal Retained State

[Paper and exact source](mathematics/analysis/affine-cycles/README.md) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23931722) · [Release](https://github.com/lmtlssss/math/releases/tag/affine-cycles-v1.0.0)

A transported cyclic cut changes the accumulated affine cost by the prefix-cost row applied to the full-cycle displacement. Exact rank formulas give the minimum linear summaries for all cut defects and all transported cut totals. An affine consistency criterion includes cut-invariant cycles without fixed points. Polynomial derivative coefficients extend the minimum-state result.
