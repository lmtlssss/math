## Mathematics · Algebra

[Exact nonnegative rank of a twelve-state composition tensor](mathematics/algebra/twelve-state-composition-tensor/)  
26 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

The nonnegative rank of a specified twelve-element monoid's composition tensor is 35. The paper gives the decomposition, matching lower bounds, and an exact verifier.

## Mathematics · Discrete mathematics

[A three-bit boundary for cyclic Boolean–modular inversion](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/three-bit-cyclic-inversion/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

Three initial bits determine every preimage of a cyclic Boolean–modular map. The complete inverse uses at most eight boundary assignments and 8w local bit updates. An explicit periodic family proves the eight-preimage bound sharp.

## Twenty signed products

[Twenty Signed Products for a Twelve-State Composition Tensor](mathematics/algebra/twenty-signed-products/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0

An explicit twenty-term signed construction for the twelve-state tensor. The real rank lies between 18 and 20. The exact nonnegative tensor-power rank is 35^q, and the signed construction uses at most 20^q products. DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23695066](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23695066).

## Mathematics · Exact counting

[Exact Counting for a Boolean–Modular Equation with Bounded Tap Span](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/bounded-tap-span-counting/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23697063](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23697063)

A fixed tap span gives a finite nonlinear frontier for exact count-and-witness solving. The manuscript proves the state bound and cyclic closure, with a 256-bit example containing 5,784,112,988,160 solutions.

## Mathematics · Affine inverse fibers

[Affine Fibers and a Fibonacci Bound for Boolean–Modular Inversion](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/affine-carry-inversion/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23697726](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23697726)

For any invertible binary-linear map, the complete Boolean–modular inverse is a disjoint union of affine cells. Two carry automata give a Fibonacci signature bound and exact one-cell families.

## Mathematics · Exact arithmetic states

[Exact State Complexity of Two-Stage Addition](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/two-stage-addition/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23698617](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23698617)

Three fixed radix-B operands define exactly 5B-4 complete two-carry response functions. Explicit normal forms attain the optimal fixed-length code, with a 35-bit code for 32-bit words and a distinct 42-bit independently queryable byte-stage contract.

## Mathematics · Cascaded sum-and-carry functions

[Exact Counts of Sum-and-Carry Functions for Cascaded Addition](mathematics/discrete-mathematics/cascaded-sum-carry/)

An exact all-stage, arbitrary-radix count and constructive minimum-length function code. Three 32-bit additions retain every incoming-carry response in exactly 36 bits.

## Mathematics · Matrix algebras

[Two Sparse Binary Operators with Optimal Generating Length](mathematics/algebra/binary-operator-algebra/)  
27 October 2026 · Version 1.0.0 · DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23701587](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23701587)

Two sparse binary operators span the full 32-by-32 matrix algebra with products of length at most ten. Ten is the minimum possible bound. A polynomial rank-one identity gives a second constructive proof.
