# Mathematical context

Gilbert and Tan (1991), DOI 10.1109/9.83532, establish maximal output-admissible-set methods. Ossareh and Kolmanovsky, arXiv:2302.02246v2 (2023), later DOI 10.1109/TAC.2024.3382601, give admissibility-index complexity bounds by matrix powers and Lyapunov methods. Stein (1952), DOI 10.6028/jres.048.010, supplies the classical quadratic-sum relation. The contribution isolated here is an exact algebraic classification of the first two possible universal horizons for the specified second-order conjugate family.

The principal result is the closed necessary-and-sufficient coefficient classification for minimal horizons two and three, including all boundary equalities. General finite determination, polar invariant sets and Stein identities are classical. The paper gives the exact second-order regions rather than presenting those general tools as new.
