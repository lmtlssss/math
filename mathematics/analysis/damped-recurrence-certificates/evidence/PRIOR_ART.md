# Mathematical context

The manuscript distinguishes its explicit constructive statements from their established foundations. References are primary publications. Classical identities are cited in the text. The note makes the stated theorem and algorithm claims under their displayed mathematical hypotheses.

Finite determination of stable output constraints is developed by Gilbert and Tan (1991), DOI 10.1109/9.83532. Stein (1952), Some General Theorems on Iterants, Journal of Research of the National Bureau of Standards 48(1), 82–83, DOI 10.6028/jres.048.010, supplies the classical discrete stability relation. This note derives the sharp two-sample coefficient, a strip-polygon formula for finite observation sets, and rational prefix/energy certificates for the specified second-order family.
