# Verification plan

## Failure modes

Zero trajectory; strict tail inequalities and tied maxima; negative real root part; nonadjacent samples with collinear rows; unbounded observation strips; rational versus floating arithmetic; finite-horizon indexing and endpoint energy.

## Principal-discovery failure modes

Negative real root part and alternating sign reduction; discriminant boundary excluded by the hypotheses; equality on hexagon facets; row versus column transition conventions; peak value versus all tie locations; second observation irredundancy.

## Acceptance

The manuscript supplies the proofs. The executable compares the derived formulas with independently evaluated whole objects. Numerical checks use integers, rational fractions or exact Gaussian-integer ranks. Worked examples produce the figure certificate. The PDF, code, citations, metadata and figure use the same definitions, coefficients and DOI. Public artifacts are mathematical and standalone.

The final figure must contain the certified objects and their exact relationships, open as a native Blender scene, and render at 3840 pixels wide. The PDF must compile without missing glyphs, unresolved references or overfull lines and all pages must pass visual inspection.
