# Principal discovery and literature comparison

The principal contributions are the common-lattice phase-selection law, its exact probability and expectation formula, and the persistent-refinement reversal theorem. Scalar monotonicity, interval elimination, homogeneity and line-envelope geometry are established foundations, not separate originality claims.

## Closest sources inspected

Dinkelbach (1967), DOI 10.1287/mnsc.13.7.492, and Schaible (1976), DOI 10.1287/opre.24.3.452, supply the fractional-programming context. Hershberger (1989), DOI 10.1016/0020-0190(89)90136-1, supplies a classical envelope algorithm. The new law concerns the linked residue events, robust availability and exact design-selection measure of a shared translating amplitude lattice. The direct cubic implementation is not an optimal envelope-complexity claim.

## Exact evidence

An exact four-design phase diagram, independent origin comparisons and 400 direct grid enumerations; 425 strict refinement-reversal comparisons; exact boundary ties and one-sided feasibility events. The manuscript gives general proofs under its explicit hypotheses; finite certificates are independent implementation evidence.

The comparison was conducted on 2 November 2026. The specific theorem statements are presented as the contributions of this preprint; the cited classical ingredients retain their attribution.
