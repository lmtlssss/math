# Principal discovery and literature comparison

The principal result is the quantitative cyclotomic spectral gap, its equality obstruction and the attained five-cycle certificate. The Fourier diagonalization, positive-semidefinite rank argument, cyclotomic irreducibility and field-norm integrality are established ingredients and receive attribution.

## Closest sources inspected

Lam and Leung (2000), DOI 10.1006/jabr.1999.8089, develop vanishing sums of roots of unity. Gray (2006), DOI 10.1561/0100000006, gives the classical circulant Fourier representation. Ragi, Chong and Mittelmann (2019), DOI 10.1109/TAES.2018.2881366, address related unimodular quadratic optimization. The present theorem combines the arithmetic norm and the integrality residue with the active spectral differences to give the displayed quantitative gap.

## Exact evidence

162 exact prime binary norm certificates; 7,172 supplementary primitive-root numerical cross-checks of exact polynomial divisibility; all 210 integer occupancies in the attained-gap example. The manuscript gives general proofs under its explicit hypotheses; finite certificates are independent implementation evidence.

The comparison was conducted on 2 November 2026. The specific theorem statements are presented as the contributions of this preprint; the cited classical ingredients retain their attribution.
