# Acceptance contract

Failure modes: a transposed multiplication order; an omitted sign; an incorrect
basis change; a false radical or quotient claim; a false nonnegative support
bound; a tensor power that denotes sequential composition; or an unsupported
claim that twenty is minimal.

Acceptance uses exact, complete coefficient identities, a separate arithmetic
implementation, full finite multiplication/representation checks, and a
written tensor-power proof. Sample-based numerical tests do not replace these.
The manuscript and released certificate must specify the same indexed monoid.
