# Attribution and scope

The construction uses established methods: Strassen's seven products for
2-by-2 matrix multiplication, the Alder–Strassen lower bound, finite algebra
representations and support-based nonnegative rank bounds.

Primary references checked on 27 October 2026:

Strassen, Gaussian elimination is not optimal, Numerische Mathematik 13
(1969), 354–356. DOI 10.1007/BF02165411.

Alder and Strassen, On the algorithmic complexity of associative algebras,
Theoretical Computer Science 15 (1981), 201–211.
DOI 10.1016/0304-3975(81)90070-0.

Bläser, A complete characterization of the algebras of minimal bilinear
complexity, SIAM Journal on Computing 34 (2005), 277–298.
DOI 10.1137/S0097539703438277. The publisher gives the issue year as 2005
and the copyright year as 2004. The citation uses the issue year.

The predecessor is the author's exact nonnegative-rank calculation, DOI
10.5281/zenodo.23678773.

Targeted searches for this twelve-element composition tensor and the exact
twenty-term factorization did not identify a matching prior construction.
These searches establish neither worldwide priority nor an optimal signed
rank. The paper records the exact instance, formulas and certificates.
